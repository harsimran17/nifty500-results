Stock
Rose about 4-6% on 5 Nov 2026, the first session after results (reports range from ₹3,735 in early trade to ₹3,842); still about 15% below its May 2026 peak.
Why
Revenue of ₹1,433 cr was about 6% above the ₹1,347 cr consensus cited by GuruFocus, and EPS of ₹19.64 was well above the ₹14.70 cited (that gap is large enough that the estimate basis may differ). Jefferies called it a modest EBITDA beat and Bernstein said earnings exceeded estimates, partly on lower Settlement Guarantee Fund contributions. Brokers are split: Motilal Oswal, HDFC Securities, PL Capital and JPMorgan are positive (targets ₹3,960-4,200), while Jefferies and Bernstein keep Underperform (₹3,100-3,180) on valuation and slowing options market-share gains.
